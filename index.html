Recent Projects

Project 1

Project Plastic

PROJECT PLASTIC, initiated by students at Agriculture College, Bapatla, focuses on sustainable solutions for plastic degradation. By studying waxworms and their plastic-degrading enzymes, the team explores genetic engineering to enhance large-scale enzyme production. With over 60 members, the project combines innovation and collaboration to address plastic pollution and promote environmental sustainability.

Project 3

Agricutural experiment

My father and I developed an organic method, using the CVR (Chintala Venkata Reddy) technique, to control fruit borers effectively. By spraying a turbid solution of deep soil and water, the pests are naturally neutralized, improving crop quality and growth. This experience ignited my passion for sustainable farming, inspiring innovative solutions for small-scale farmers.

Fast Memorizing tool

I developed an app that helps students prepare smarter for exams by leveraging neuroscience principles like the Forgetting Curve and Pareto’s Principle.
